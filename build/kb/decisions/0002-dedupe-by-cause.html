Context

The rendered #4471 review shows distinct parser and form-hint causes merged into one ticket. Subject-only matching in decision 0001 combines different bugs and separates equivalent reports with different wording. Intake tags also disagree with tenant-based classification.

Proposed replacement for decision 0001

Resolve the tenant’s locale and booking-form version, then inspect the channel and failure mechanism. Merge reports only when their causes match. A matching subject is insufficient; a different subject does not prevent a merge. Missing tenant or cause information requires investigation, not an automatic merge. Keep a report once per ID and attach all IDs to the cause’s ticket.
