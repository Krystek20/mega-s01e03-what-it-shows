Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk under review reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

A busy morning brings hundreds of reports about the same thing. We keep one ticket per issue and merge every duplicate into it, so engineering sees one thread with a count.

The rule

A report is a duplicate when its verified cause matches an open ticket. The #4471 review contradicts the old subject-only rule in decision 0001. Decision 0002 proposes its replacement:

  1. resolve the tenant, locale, booking-form version and report channel;
  2. inspect the failure mechanism, using the component’s classification as evidence;
  3. merge into a ticket for the same verified cause, regardless of subject wording;
  4. count each report ID once and preserve conflicting or unexplained symptoms.
Matching subjects do not establish matching causes. Pause automatic subject-only merges while the support desk reviews the replacement decision.

When you are unsure

Hold for investigation. Missing tenant information is not evidence of a shared cause. Request the input, expected and observed dates, locale, form version and timestamps before merging. Escalate symptoms the classifier does not explain.
